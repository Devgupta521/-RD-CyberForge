01 — About
Who We Are

The people behind RD CyberForge.

DG
Devendra Gupta
Founder @ RD CyberForge | Cybersecurity Trainer | Aspiring SOC Analyst | Digital Forensics & Incident Response | VAPT | Treasurer @ HHA_SVCE

I am a Cybersecurity enthusiast and B-Tech student specializing in Cyber Security, with hands-on experience in Security Operations, Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT), Network Security, and Web Application Security.

During my cybersecurity internships, I gained practical exposure to reconnaissance, vulnerability assessment, network enumeration, exploitation, OWASP Top 10 vulnerabilities, Burp Suite, Nmap, Metasploit, Wireshark, SQLMap, and other security tools. I have also completed major projects involving Network Security Assessment and Web Application Security Assessment.

I am currently focused on building my career as a SOC Analyst, developing skills in security monitoring, log analysis, SIEM, incident response, threat detection, and MITRE ATT&CK. I am also interested in Digital Forensics and contributing to cybercrime investigation and law enforcement efforts in the future.

I believe in continuous learning through hands-on labs, cybersecurity projects, CTFs, and real-world security scenarios. My goal is to grow as a cybersecurity professional and contribute to building a safer digital environment.

I am Rajyalakshmi, currently pursuing my 3rd year B.Tech in Computer Science Engineering, specializing in Cybersecurity, with a keen interest in Cybersecurity, AI, and emerging technologies.
